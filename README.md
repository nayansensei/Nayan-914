## About Me

I'm a first-year **Computer Engineering (BCT)** student at **Sagarmatha Engineering College, Tribhuvan University**, based in **Kathmandu, Nepal**.

I learn by **shipping, not staying stuck in tutorial hell**. Right now I'm building the **Sensei BCT System** while locking down strong fundamentals in **C programming**, how computers work under the hood, and writing code every single week.

Outside engineering, I edit **anime and gaming videos** for an international audience — which taught me the other half of building things: **packaging, storytelling, and making people actually care about what you made.**

> ***Creating Greatness.*** — that's the standard I work by.

## Featured Project — Sensei BCT System

A centralized study platform built for **IOE BCT** students — every subject, chapter, note, old question, and question bank in **one place**, with everything opening directly inside the app.

* **Full Course Hierarchy:** `Home → Semester → Subject → Chapter` structured cleanly for the entire BCT syllabus
* **Exam-Focused Resources:** Notes, past papers, and question banks built around the real IOE pattern
* **Zero Friction:** Built because scattered Google Drive folders and dead links waste study time
