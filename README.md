<h2>Featured Project</h2>

<h3>Sensei BCT System</h3>

<p>A study platform for BCT students — every subject, chapter, notes, old questions and question bank in <b>one place</b>, with everything opening inside the app.</p>

<ul>
  <li>Home → Semester → Subject → Chapter structure for the full BCT course</li>
  <li>Notes, old questions and a question bank following the real IOE pattern</li>
  <li>Everything opens inside the app — no scattered Drive folders, no redirect maze</li>
  <li>Built because scattered folders and dead links waste study time</li>
</ul>
